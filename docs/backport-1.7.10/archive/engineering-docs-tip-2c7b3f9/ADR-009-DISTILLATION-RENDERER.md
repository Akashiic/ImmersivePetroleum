# ADR-009 - Distillation Tower renderer foundation

Status: **ACCEPTED for G5C1 / alpha.9**
Date: 2026-09-12

## Decision
The first Distillation Tower renderer is a clean-room procedural TESR owned only by the canonical formed master. It ships no copied Immersive Petroleum model, OBJ, texture or Blender asset.

The renderer reuses the target Immersive Engineering block atlas/material icons and builds an industrial tower silhouette from procedural primitives: foundation skid, lower vessel, twelve-sided column shell, reinforcement bands, platforms, rails, ladder, process riser, takeoffs, gauges and an active heat layer.

Client implementation remains isolated behind FML `@SidedProxy`; dedicated-server loading must never resolve LWJGL, Tessellator or the client renderer.
## Dispatch and culling
Only a formed master is TESR-eligible. Non-master parts return zero render distance and a local 1x1x1 AABB. The master uses a conservative whole-tower envelope spanning the procedural geometry through approximately 17 blocks of vertical extent.

The larger master envelope is intentional. One master renderer is cheap, while an overly tight box can create culling regressions with Angelica. Tightening is deferred until a measured LOD/culling campaign demonstrates value.

## Performance policy
The renderer emits a few thousand vertices per visible tower frame through Tessellator/fixed-function-compatible paths. No display-list/VBO cache is added at this gate: the current cost is modest and Angelica already virtualizes fixed-function state, making another caching layer higher risk than value.

GL state ownership is narrow: matrix, texture binding, color and culling only. Culling/color are restored after every draw; the renderer does not alter blend or depth-test policy globally.
## Angelica validation boundary
The renderer is validated in the MCP development runtime with Angelica 2.2.8-dev, GTNHLib 0.11.41-dev and UniMixins 0.3.1-dev on Intel UHD Graphics / OpenGL 4.6 with Angelica FFP emulation active.

The probe covers four horizontal facings x mirrored/unmirrored x inactive/active (16 matrix draws) plus five additional active draws. Every audited draw requires `glGetError()==GL_NO_ERROR`, and `GL_CULL_FACE` must be restored exactly.

Visual evidence is generated by installing a formed master into the scratch client world and letting Minecraft's normal TileEntity dispatcher render it for multiple frames before capture. This replaced an earlier transient manual-draw screenshot method whose framebuffer timing was non-deterministic.

## Deferred work
G5C1 approves the renderer foundation only. Final original art fidelity, polished emissive/material treatment, active third-party shaderpack certification, GUI/NEI/CraftTweaker presentation and final LOD tuning remain later gates.
